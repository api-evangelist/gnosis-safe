Managing transactions on Safe just got easier. With Proposers, Safe owners can now allow trusted non-signers to prepare transactions without granting full signing authority. This update is designed to streamline high-volume workflows, making transaction management simpler and more efficient, especially for teams handling grants, incentives, or frequent payments.What Are Proposers?The Proposer feature enables selected users to initiate transactions for review and approval by Safe’s signers.
